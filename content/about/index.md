I am Yan Wan, studying M2 Virtual & Augmented Reality at Institut Polytechnique de Paris.

This website is an academic portfolio for the **2026–2027** year. It brings together assignments, lab work, experiments, and projects across all ten courses in the programme.

The aim is to document not just finished results but also the process behind them: questions, technical decisions, prototypes, difficulties, solutions, and reflections.

The Human-Computer Interface section already contains published work. The other course sections have been created as spaces to add material throughout the year.
