This website is a course portfolio for Human-Computer Interaction. It documents lecture-based homework and lab work as a sequence of short, visual case studies.

The goal is not only to present final results, but also to explain the design reasoning behind them: what I observed, which HCI principle is involved, why the design succeeds or fails, and how it could be improved.
